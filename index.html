01

About

I started building in Class 10 with a Li-Fi system — sending audio through light because I wanted to find out whether it would actually work outside a textbook.

Since then I've been building AI tools for students: research, learning, and choosing universities. I care about this because good guidance is expensive. A counsellor can cost more than a family spends on a term of school, and most students never get one.

Most of what I know came from breaking things in public, on GitHub.

VEDA

In progress

Virtual Engine for Discovery & Analysis — an AI research assistant

github.com/purveshnileshbhadale/veda ↗ 50 commits My most active project
Problem
Papers arrive faster than anyone can read them. Finding the gap between two of them is slow, manual work that most students do badly or skip.
Built
Literature search, PDF upload, arXiv integration, AI analysis of a paper, and knowledge graphs that map how ideas connect — so a gap becomes something you can see instead of something you hope to notice.
Tech
Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind, Python FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Docker. Runs on free-tier LLM APIs (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek) — a design decision that kept the project affordable and forced me to handle rate limits properly.
Proof
Public repo with 50 commits — my most active project by a wide margin. TODO — screenshot or demo video, plus one real result from a real query

SCHOLARS

University shortlisting from a student profile, starting with Australia

github.com/purveshnileshbhadale/SCHOLARS ↗ 29 commits ≈1,500 universities in the dataset

Built
A shortlist generated from a student's profile — grades, budget, course interest — starting with Australia.
